Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Grafaxis, an image editor for Android, handles information. Grafaxis is built so that your work stays on your device: it does not collect any personal or non-personal data, and it cannot connect to the internet.

Because nothing is collected, our practices are simple, and this policy is mostly a description of what the app keeps on your own device and how you stay in control of it.

Personal Data Collection and Use

Grafaxis does not collect, store on any server, process, share or sell any personal data or non-personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no user profiles.

Personal data is any information that could identify you as an individual. Non-personal data is information that does not directly identify you, such as aggregated or anonymised usage statistics. Grafaxis collects neither, so there is nothing we could use, share or sell.

No Internet Access

Grafaxis does not request Android's internet permission, so the app is unable to send or receive anything over a network. It contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting software. Fonts, images and projects are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

To work as an editor, Grafaxis saves the following in its private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Your projects — layers, shapes, text, colours, effects and settings — together with automatic recovery copies and preview thumbnails.
  • Images you import into a project. Grafaxis keeps a copy of the picture itself; the photo's metadata, such as camera details or the location where it was taken, is not kept.
  • Fonts you import into the font library.
  • App preferences, such as the theme, recently used colours, the colour picker you prefer, snapping options and the last export format.

This information never leaves your device through the app. You can remove it at any time by deleting projects or fonts inside Grafaxis, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Files and Permissions

Grafaxis requests no runtime permissions: it does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or the rest of your device storage. When you import an image or a font, or export your work, Android's own file picker opens, and Grafaxis can only read or write the files you choose there, at that moment.

Files you export — PNG, JPEG, PDF or SVG — are newly created from your project. They contain your artwork and the technical information needed to display it, such as size, resolution and colour space; an SVG file also carries the names you gave the project and its layers, so that it stays editable in other apps. Exported files never carry the metadata of photos you imported, and where they are saved is always your choice.

Device Backup

If you have turned on backup for your device, Android may include your Grafaxis projects, imported fonts and project list in your device backup to your Google account, and in transfers when you move to a new device. These backups are made by Android and handled under Google's terms; the developer of Grafaxis has no access to them. Recovery copies and thumbnails are excluded. You can turn backup off at any time in your device's settings.

Security

Since Grafaxis does not collect or transmit any data, there is no data in transit or on our servers to protect. Your work is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and, on most devices, by Android's storage encryption. We still treat the security of the app and the trust you place in it as a priority.

Third-Party Data Collection

While Grafaxis itself does not collect any data, you may still be subject to data collection by third parties, such as the store you installed the app from (for example Google Play), your operating system, your device manufacturer, and other apps with permission to access your device. We have no control over, and take no responsibility for, their practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Grafaxis does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, and can be used without providing any personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date, and take effect as soon as they are posted.
